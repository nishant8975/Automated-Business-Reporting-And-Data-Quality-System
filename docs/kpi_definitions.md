# KPI Definitions

The following table documents the definitions, calculations, and exact business meaning of the core Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) calculated by this pipeline. 

| Metric | Definition | Source Table / View | Calculation | Business Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Total Orders** | Count of distinct orders. | `clean.orders` / `analytics.v_order_kpis` | `COUNT(DISTINCT order_id)` | The total volume of transactions initiated by customers. |
| **Total Customers** | Count of distinct human customers. | `clean.customers` / `analytics.v_order_kpis` | `COUNT(DISTINCT customer_unique_id)` | The size of the actual customer base. (Uses `customer_unique_id` instead of transaction-bound `customer_id`). |
| **Repeat Customers** | Customers who have placed more than one order. | `clean.customers` & `clean.orders` / `analytics.v_order_kpis` | `COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN order_count > 1 THEN customer_unique_id END)` | A measure of customer loyalty and retention. |
| **GMV** | Gross Merchandise Value (excluding freight). | `clean.order_items` / `analytics.v_order_kpis` | `SUM(price)` | The core primary revenue metric for items sold on the platform. |
| **Total Freight** | Total shipping charges collected. | `clean.order_items` / `analytics.v_order_kpis` | `SUM(freight_value)` | Shipping logistics revenue. |
| **Merchandise + Freight** | The total cart value. | `clean.order_items` / `analytics.v_order_kpis` | `SUM(price + freight_value)` | The total value of the shopping cart generated by the items and their shipping. |
| **AOV** | Average Order Value (Merchandise only). | `clean.order_items` & `clean.orders` / `analytics.v_order_kpis` | `SUM(price) / COUNT(DISTINCT order_id)` | The average merchandise value a customer spends per order. |
| **Items Sold** | Total physical products sold. | `clean.order_items` / `analytics.v_order_kpis` | `COUNT(*)` | The absolute volume of products moving through the system. |
| **Average Review Score** | Mean satisfaction score (1-5). | `clean.order_reviews` / `analytics.v_order_kpis` | `AVG(review_score)` | Overall customer satisfaction based on post-purchase surveys. |
| **Payment Value** | Total funds processed by payment gateways. | `clean.order_payments` / `analytics.v_payment_analysis` | `SUM(payment_value)` | Financial metric for gateway reconciliation. *Note: Payment value exceeds merchandise + freight by $165,130.25. The current dataset does not provide sufficient evidence to attribute this difference to specific factors, so payment value is retained as a separate payment/reconciliation metric rather than being used as the primary GMV measure.* |
